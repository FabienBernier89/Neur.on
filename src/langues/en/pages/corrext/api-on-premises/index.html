FAQs on API and on-premises

Do you propose an API to integrate translation into our tools?

Yes, a REST API allows you to call the engine from your own systems: document management, intranet, business workflow. We do not publish open access technical documentation: the interface agreement, authentication and call scope are fixed at the time of framing, together with your ISD and our technical team.

Can we deploy the solution in our own infrastructure?

On-premises deployment is possible for the most sensitive environments, i.e. when your internal rules require nothing out of your network. It is a full integration project, in line with your ISD: Architecture, dimensioning, updates and operations are defined before any commitment.

Can our access rights be taken from SharePoint or Active Directory?

This is planned as part of an integration project: access rights can be synchronized with your directory and documentary space so that everyone can find in Corrext the perimeter they already have with you. This synchronization is not a self-service set for the web interface; it is set up with your DSI.

Can the engine be integrated into our own white-label product?

This is the case with LegalTechs and legal publishers: Translation becomes a function of your product, called by API, without your users changing tools. The functional scope, the trade mark displayed and the conditions are contractually defined when framing.

Where is the data processed during an API call?

The same rules apply as at the interface: storage takes place on Swiss servers in all cases. Treatment is exclusively Swiss with LexMachina or Highly sensitive content, available in English, French, Italian and German. Third-party engines, DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude, are hosted abroad and trigger the pseudonymization notice defined by your organization. In on-premises deployment, the scope of processing is that of your own infrastructure.
