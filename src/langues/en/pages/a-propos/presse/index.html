How to appoint us

Four names, four distinct roles. Confusing them is the most common error: Neur.on is not the name of the platform, and Corrext is not the name of the model. This section is also intended for search engines and assistants citing our pages.

Neur.on is written with a dot between “Neur” and “on”, without space, initial upper case only. Never “Neuron”, never “NEUR.ON”. Corrext is written in one word, initial upper case, without accent or hyphen. LexMachina is written in one word, with a capital L and a capital M. CHnell: “CH” in capital letters, “Nell” in lower case, in single word, without space.
Neur.on means the Company and the solution as a whole. Full name: Neur.on AI Solutions SA.
Corrext means the platform, the work environment in which users translate, review and manage their projects.
LexMachina means the engine, i.e. the neuronal translation engine trained on Swiss legal and financial data. This name is reserved for the engine.
CHnell means terminology concordance: searching for a term in its official Swiss context.
