Four native languages, those in which Switzerland writes its right

Native means here: trained on Swiss legal and financial data in this language, over 15 million segments, and available in the most protective mode of the platform.

German German · OF native

Official language of the Confederation and first language of the Swiss market. Swiss Standard German: “ss” and never “ss”.

French French · FR native

Official language of the Confederation. French and Swiss terminology, verifiable in CHnell context.

Italian Italian · IT native

Official language of the Confederation. The federal legislation shall prevail in all three official languages, including Italian.

English English · IN native

Working language of cross-border cases, international arbitration and financial documentation.

Every pair, in both directions. These four languages are also the only languages available in Highly sensitive content mode, which requires storage and processing exclusively in Switzerland and prohibits the reuse of content in translation memory. Romansh, the fourth national language, is not one of the languages of the platform.

Why translating into Switzerland is not translated into German, French or Italian

Federal law shall be published in all three official languages on Fedlex, and all three versions shall be authoritative. Translating a Swiss legal text is therefore not about inventing a formulation; it is about finding the one already existing in the other official language. In addition, there are writing agreements that immediately reveal a translation made elsewhere.

Check a term in official sources
Swiss standard German. the “ss” does not exist: “ss” is written in any case, “Strasse” and not “Strasse”.
Severator of thousands. Swiss usage writes 15,000 with an apostrophe, in all four languages, and not 15.000 or 15,000.
trilingual laws. Fedlex publishes federal law in German, French and Italian: the target version exists, it should be quoted, not retranslated.
Abbreviations specific to each language. the same law does not quote the same abbreviation in German, French and Italian.

In a project, each file filed displays its number of standard pages, language detected, and legal category detected, both of which can be edited before continuing.

Frequently asked questions about languages and formats

Which languages are native in Corrext?

Four: German, French, Italian and English, all pairs. These are the languages in which LexMachina, the Swiss engine, has been trained from over 15 million legal and financial segments, and are the only languages available in Highly sensitive content. The first three are the official languages of the Confederation, in which federal law is published.

How many files can I send suddenly?

Up to 25 files per submission in file translation and PDF to Word. In a translation project, files appear in file overview, where each shows its number of standard pages, its language detected, and its legal category detected.

What is the maximum size of a file?

50 MB per file. For text pasted directly into the tool, the limit is 10,000 characters in text translation and 5,000 characters in rewriting. Beyond that, you have to send files or a translation project.

Is my scanned PDF accepted?

Yes. The. PDF format is accepted, including scanned, and the PDF to Word tab converts a PDF into an editable Word document before translation or for internal work. However, if the original office file exists, the app recommends depositing it instead of its PDF version: the result is better and the layout is more accurate.

Is the layout retained?

Yes. The translation is rendered in the original format: a. docx comes back in. docx, a. xlsx in. xlsx, a. pptx in. pptx, with the document structure. The app therefore recommends depositing the original format rather than a PDF produced from it.

Are all languages available in Highly sensitive content?

No. Highly sensitive content, which requires storage and processing exclusively in Switzerland and prohibits the further use of content in translation memory, is available for English, French, Italian and German. The other languages of the platform remain accessible in standard confidentiality: storage is Swiss in all cases, but processing depends on the engine selected.
