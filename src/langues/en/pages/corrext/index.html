Frequently asked questions about Corrext

What is Corrext?

Corrext is Neur.on's legal and financial translation platform. It combines four tools in a single Swiss environment: Text translation and document, project management, fast lookup CHnell and certified commercial register extracts.

Where are my documents stored and processed?

Corrext is housed in Switzerland at INFOMANIAK. The documents are processed in Switzerland in Highly sensitive content or using the LexMachina engine. Engines hosted abroad trigger your organization's privacy notice and are not available in Highly sensitive content. Neur.on is ISO 27001 certified.

Which translation engines can I use?

LexMachina, DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT or Claude, at any time when sent. Corrext can display up to four alternatives, each attributed to its engine, to compare before choosing.

Which formats and languages does the Corrext accept?

Seven types of office files, from PDF to PowerPoint, up to 25 50 MB files per submission, and pasted text up to 10,000 characters. German, French, Italian and English are native languages; the platform offers thirty languages in total.

does Corrext replace the replay with a translator?

No. For a binding document, project management requires a review: light review or full review by a legal translator, or double review and certified translation. The quotation and time period are displayed before validation.

How does Corrext integrate with our tools?

By a native MS Word plugin or browser application, by a REST API for your DMS and intranets, or by on-premises deployment. Access rights are set up to the document, through SharePoint or Active Directory.
