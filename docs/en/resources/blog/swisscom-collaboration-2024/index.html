Neur.on and Swisscom announce a collaboration for the legal and banking sectors

Neur.on team 29 November 2024 1 min read
Share
In brief

Swisscom and Neur.on officially announced a collaboration at the Kickstart Innovation 2024 closing ceremony. The two companies are launching a PoC: a secure legal and financial translation and drafting toolbox for Swiss banks and law firms, hosted in a 100% Swiss environment.

Very big news for Swiss law firms and banks.

Last night, at the Kickstart Innovation 2024 closing ceremony, Stefan Koller of Swisscom and Neur.on officially announced a collaboration to better serve the legal and banking sectors. Together, we are launching a PoC (proof of concept) to build the most secure legal and financial translation and drafting toolbox for lawyers and finance professionals in Switzerland.

The solution will allow Swiss banks and law firms to draw on the power of Neur.on’s specialised LLMs, both with their own data (stored with full security) and with Neur.on’s unique, carefully curated multilingual datasets specific to these fields. All hosted in a 100% Swiss environment.

Thank you, Swisscom, and in particular Silvan Lohri and Daniel Dobos, for the honour of being among the first companies to deploy on your brand-new, high-end NVIDIA GPU infrastructure. Our engineers are thrilled by its processing power and by the wide range of options for calibrating security.

Special thanks also to Katka Letzing, Elisabetta Maria Chiarantoni and the whole Kickstart team for accelerating the launch of our PoC.

The fun has only just begun.
