Everything you need to know

Further answers in help Center and in our resources: Glossary, guides and studies.

How does Neur.on ensure the accuracy of Swiss law?

LexMachina, our driver, is trained in over 15 million Swiss legal and financial segments: ATF/BGE, FINMA texts, codes (CO, CC, LP). You can compare up to four alternatives to other motors, check each term in its official context with CHnell, and request a review by a lawyer-linguist.

Do my data remain sovereign?

You choose, project by project. In Standard Mode, your data is stored on Swiss Corrext servers and processed by the engine you select: LexMachina in Switzerland; DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT or Claude abroad, with a warning of pseudonymisation. In Highly sensitive mode, storage and processing remain exclusively in Switzerland, with no further use in memory. All ISO 27001 certified, nLPD and GDPR compliant.

Which languages and areas are covered?

Thirty languages on the platform, with native German, French, Italian and English (mode highly sensitive and LexMachina). CHnell covers 30 areas, from banking and FINMA supervision to arbitration, taxation, intellectual property or company law. For certified, notarized or apostised translations, Corrext offers a dedicated online service.

How does DeepL differ from a general LLM?

Generic tools translate text without knowing Swiss law or its official sources, and host your data outside of Switzerland. Corrext lets you use it when relevant, within a governed framework, and sets aside a driver powered by Swiss law, comparison of alternatives, terminology verification in context, and a 100% Swiss mode.

Is the platform agnostic with AI models?

Yes. In Corrext, you choose the engine for each translation: LexMachina, DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT or Claude, and you compare up to four engine alternatives. It's a long-term solution that changes with the state of the art and does n't lock you up.

How can we integrate Neur.on with our existing tools?

Via the Microsoft Word plugin, the Corrext web application, or the REST API for your DMS, intranets, and workflows. Rights management is granular, up to document level (SharePoint/AD). On-premises deployment is possible. Book a demo to match the integration with an expert.
