“Technology empowers, not Overpowers”

Our leitmotif holds in one sentence, and it decides everything else. A legal translation incurs liability: that of the lawyer who signs it, not that of a model. We are therefore building tools that increase the legal professional, give him the context, official terminology and alternatives he needs to decide quickly, and leave him the decision. An engine that would decide in its stead would not be a step forward; it would be a risk transfer.

That is why we never announce the disappearance of the translator or legal linguist. The following four levels of quality are a concrete translation of this principle: you choose the machine share and the human share that is at stake, and you always know which one you chose.

Four levels of quality, because not all documents undertake the same thing

The portion of human beings is selected document by document. This is the most concrete form that our principle takes.

LEVEL 1

Machine Translation

To understand quickly: document review, internal reading, file sorting. The engine alone, without re-reading, and you know that.

LEVEL 2

Post-Edition

Light or full: contracts, General terms and conditions, correspondence. A lawyer-linguist links for a publication.

LEVEL 3

Legal review

For high stakes: legal opinions, publication, binding documents. The re-examination is expert and assumed.

LEVEL 4

Certification and apostille

Certified and authenticated translation of official files via a network of expert translators.

entity

infrastructure

Accommodation at INFOMANIAK, Switzerland. Your documents are stored on Swiss servers in any case, outside the scope of the US CLOUD act, in accordance with the revFADP and GDPR. ISO 27001 certification obtained in February 2024.

Engine

LexMachina is trained on Swiss legal and financial data: ATF/BGE, FINMA texts, codes. More than 15 million multilingual segments, not a general model to which a glossary would have been added.

treatment

Exclusively in Switzerland with LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content. Third-party engines, hosted abroad, remain available but trigger the pseudonymization notice defined by your organization.

Quadrilingualism

German, French, Italian and English native, in all pairs, and 30 languages on the platform. Federal law is equally authentic in German, French and Italian, and international matters are handled in English: a Swiss legal tool cannot treat any of these languages as secondary.
