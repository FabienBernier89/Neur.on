What we rule out

Three rules that explain why you will not find a spectacular ranking here.

No score without a published corpus

A percentage without a corpus, a date and a protocol means nothing. Until the corpus is described, no figure is published.

No hidden comparisons

The engines compared are named, their versions are dated, and the faulty outputs of our own engine appear alongside the others.

No raw speed measurements

Speed depends on load and network. It says nothing about the accuracy of a statutory reference, the only criterion that counts in a binding document.
