What general LLMs do remarkably well

These are the best tools ever handed over to a lawyer to work on the meaning of a text. Saying so does not weaken our argument: that is the honest starting point for the comparison.

  • reformulate and simplify transform a dense clause into a paragraph readable for a non-legal client, or vice versa, keeping the registry requested.
  • summarize and explain distil the structure of a fifty-page brief, explain a contractual mechanism, isolate the obligations of each party.
  • hold ambiguity propose several reads of an ambiguous formulation and explain what separates them, where a conventional translation engine writes without saying so.
  • Editorial Explorer quickly produce multiple variations of a clause to feed an internal discussion before stopping the version that will be negotiated.

What they cannot prove on their own

The problem is not the quality of the language; it is excellent. The problem is that none of their claims goes back to a source that you can show to a colleague or authority.

  • No sourced Swiss terminology a term is rendered plausibly, without any indication of how the Federal or fedlex.admin.ch Gazette actually renders it. In Corrext, this is the role of fast lookup CHnell.
  • No warranty of legal references an article, abbreviation or jurisdiction may be produced with lead and forgery, without any signal in the response.
  • No preview interface No validation string: no preview level, no ordered legal translator, no certification possible. In Corrext, it commands in Project Management.
  • No quotation, no deadline, no governance There is nothing that makes it possible to make a distinction between understanding quickly and reading, and nothing that reminds the cabinet's policy before sending a document out of Switzerland.

An example of error, and that is our case

A contract law clause translated from French to German in Corrext. Two alternatives in the same panel do not say the same thing, and one of them changes the meaning of the clause.

Source · French

The debtor in default shall owe default interest at the rate of 5% per annum in accordance with CO Art. 104 para. 1, without the need for formal notice when a term has been agreed.

wrongful exit · LexMachina

Der säumige Schuldner schuldet gemäss Art. 104 ABS. 1 OR Verzugszinsen in Höhe von 5% p.a., ohne DASS eine förmliche Mahnung erforderlich wäre, wenn eine Kündigung vereinbart worden IST.

alternative of the same panel · Azure OpenAI GPT

Der Schuldner im Verzug schuldet Verzugszinsen zum Satz von 5% pro Jahr gemäss Art. 104 ABS. 1 OHNE DASS eine förmliche Mahnung erforderlich IST, wenn eine Frist vereinbart wurde.

Kündigung refers to the termination of the contract, whereas the agreed “term” is a term, which the legal translator checks in the German text of the CO. The mistake comes from our own engine, not from a competitor, and it illustrates exactly what we support here: no generalist or specialized engine is a source of law. What protects the reader is not the promise of an infallible model, it is the alternatives displayed side by side, the concordage that goes back to the official source, and human reinterpretation before any transmission.

Actual exits obtained in Corrext, on this precise phrase, in French to German, in the alternatives panel. One-time, dated example, not a measure: this type of error occurs in all engines, and another generation would give another result. We kept it unchanged rather than regenerating it, because it documents the real risk.

Frequently asked questions about general AML and Swiss law

Can a contract be translated by ChatGPT?

To understand a clause, understand its scope or prepare a discussion, yes: a general LLM is doing very well. For a contract to be signed, forwarded to a third party or produced before an authority, no, not alone: it does not provide any sourced Swiss terminology, guarantee of legal references, reinterpretation by a legal translator, or data processing in Switzerland. In Corrext, these templates remain available, but the release that begins requires a light, full, or double review.

How can I verify a Swiss legal benchmark?

Don't ask the engine that just produced it: ask a source. In Corrext, follow the term or abbreviation in the source text and click fast Lookup. The CHnell concordance shows aligned segments drawn from official Swiss sources, with the domain, source and a Show in context link. Then you see how the term is actually rendered in the Federal Gazette or on the fedlex.admin.ch, and not how a model presupposes that it should be.

does Corrext use general LLMs?

Yes. Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude are available in Corrext, alongside LexMachina and DeepL Pro, and choose for each shipment. As they are hosted abroad, their selection triggers the Attorney-Client privilege opinion of your organization, which requires full pseudonymization and prohibits these engines for sensitive files. The alternatives panel compares their proposals with those of the Swiss engine in the same sentence.

Does a general LLM create legal provisions?

It may produce a plausible but false reference, jurisdiction or term, and nothing in its response indicates the error. This risk does not relate to a particular supplier: we documented it on our own engine, on a default clause issued in German with Kündigung, which refers to termination where the agreed maturity was required. This is precisely why Corrext has several engine-based alternatives and gives access to the concordance tree.

Are my discussions with a general public IA assistant confidential?

The conditions vary from supplier to supplier and offer, and they change. Two things remain constant for a Swiss firm: the document is transferred outside of Switzerland, and nothing in the interface reminds the User of what his/her internal policy requires before sending it. In Corrext, Attorney-Client privilege is displayed when the engine is selected, and Highly sensitive content requires storage and processing exclusively in Switzerland.

When is a general LLM the right tool?

When you work on meaning rather than final form: summarize a long document, explain a contractual mechanism, reformulate a paragraph for a non-legal client, explore several editors of a clause. Corrext proposes its own rewriting function, Rephrasing, in five styles including Formal legal, Financial and simplified.
