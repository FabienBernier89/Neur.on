What sets a specialised engine apart from a general-purpose one?

Three things you can measure on your own texts: the terminology used in Swiss acts published in German, French and Italian; the exact form of legal references, which changes with the language; and consistency from one document to the next. That is exactly what a legal expert checks first.

What LexMachina is trained on
Official terminology. The equivalents come from legislation published in the Confederation's three official languages.
Legal references. Abbreviations change with the language: the Code of Obligations is CO in French, OR in German and CO in Italian.
Verifiability. Every term opens in context, with the official source behind it.

What Neur.on brings to a third-party product

A legal publisher does not need one more engine. It needs an engine its users will not catch out on a reference to the Code of Obligations. That is the only ground on which Neur.on asks to be compared.

Frequently asked questions from publishers and LegalTechs

Can the engine be integrated by API?

Yes, a REST API lets you connect translation to your products, document management systems and intranets. The technical scope is defined in a dedicated discussion.

Is deployment on our servers possible?

Yes, on-premises deployment is available for the most sensitive environments.

What is the engine trained on?

On Swiss legal and financial data: Federal Supreme Court case law, FINMA texts and codes, more than 15 million multilingual segments in all.

Are we tied to a single engine?

No. The platform is engine-agnostic: it orchestrates LexMachina, DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude, and the Alternatives feature compares their output.

Can we offer the service as a white label?

Integration terms, including white-label options, are defined case by case in a technical and commercial discussion.

What security guarantees can we give our clients?

Hosting in Switzerland, ISO 27001 certification obtained in February 2024, compliance with the revised FADP and the GDPR, and processing in Switzerland only in Highly sensitive content mode or with the LexMachina engine.
