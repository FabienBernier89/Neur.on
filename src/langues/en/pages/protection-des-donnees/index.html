1. Data collected and rights of data subjects

When you visit our website, we collect various data, some of which are personal data. You have the following rights to the personal data we hold about you:

  • the right to ask us for information about the personal data we hold about you;
  • the right to have us correct or delete such data;
  • the right to object to or restrict the processing of your personal data.

If our processing is based on your consent, you may revoke it at any time, without stating any reason, by sending a written request to the address indicated above. Please note that we may only process such a request after we have obtained proof of your identity.

2. Collection of general data

Whenever you visit our pages, we collect a series of general data. This general data and information is saved in the server logs. The following data shall be collected:

  • IP address;
  • date and time of the request;
  • time lag relative to the GMT Zone;
  • content of the application;
  • HTTP status code and access status;
  • the quantity of data transmitted;
  • webpage from which the request originates;
  • browser (including language and version)
  • operating system.

The collected general data is not attributed to an identified person. We need to collect them for technical reasons in order to spread our website in a stable and secure way.

3. Contact form

When you send us a request using the contact form, we record the data you enter into the form, including your contact details, in order to process your request. The processing of this information is governed by our Privacy Policy, a copy of which can be obtained upon request and the link to which is provided in each quotation we prepare for you. We will not pass this data on to third parties without your consent.

The information entered in the contact form is stored only as long as it serves its purpose. Where their storage is based solely on your consent, we will delete them as soon as you withdraw that consent, unless the law requires a longer retention period.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies, i.e. small text files stored on your computer that contain searchable information about your browsing behavior. Cookies are used to establish your connection to our web services and to personalise your browsing. They collect information about your IP address, date and time of your visit, number of visits, forms used, search settings, pages displayed and preference settings on our site.

Most of the cookies we use are “session cookies”, automatically deleted at the end of each visit. Other cookies remain stored on your computer until you delete them. You can configure your browser to prevent our site from installing cookies, or to disable cookies in general. You can also ask your browser to delete all cookies already installed at any time. In this case, your settings may prevent our site from functioning properly.
