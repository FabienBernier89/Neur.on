What Fast lookup CHnell does, exactly

Fast lookup CHnell is the bilingual concordancer in Corrext. It searches for a word, an expression or a term in aligned segments from official Swiss sources and your own resources. It highlights the term in both languages and cites the domain and source of each occurrence. Everything below was observed in the application.

Frequently asked questions about Fast lookup CHnell

What is a bilingual concordancer?

A tool that shows a segment in the source language side by side with its translation as published. CHnell highlights the search term in both languages and gives the domain and source of each segment, for example fedlex.admin.ch or the Federal Gazette. You see how a term is actually translated in official texts, not just how it could be.

Which sources feed CHnell?

Official Swiss texts: Fedlex (fedlex.admin.ch), the Federal Gazette (Bundesblatt), and publications of federal authorities such as the Federal Tax Administration (estv.admin.ch) and fedpol (fedpol.admin.ch), brought together in the Neur.on data sets. Your organisation's own resources are added, kept separate from the shared resources.

Which domains are covered?

Thirty domains, including Banking & Finance, Supervisory (FINMA), Fund Prospectuses, Company Law, Articles of Association, M&A, Accounting, Contracts & GTCs, Data Protection, Real Estate, Criminal Law, Litigation & Enforcement, Arbitration, Employment & Social Ins., Tax & Customs, Administrative Law, Intellectual Property, Med & Pharma, Environment and Trade. Each segment carries its domain, and the Domains filter lets you combine several.

Which search operators are available?

Three operators, shown below the search field: quotation marks "", the - sign and or. They work as in common search engines: exact phrase, exclusion of a word, alternative between two terms. You search for a word, an expression or a term and start the search with the Search button.

Can I add my own resources?

In the Resource type filter, the application distinguishes Own resources from Shared resources: your organisation's own resources are searched separately from the shared data sets. A Neur.on expert shows you how to set up your resources in a demo.

How do I use CHnell without leaving a translation?

In Text and document translation, highlight a word in the source text: a Fast Lookup button appears and opens CHnell on that word, in context, with the aligned segments and the domain and source of each occurrence. You then return to your translation.
