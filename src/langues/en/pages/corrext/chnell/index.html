What fast lookup CHnell does exactly

Fast lookup CHnell is the bilingual Corrext concordance. He looks for a word, expression or term in aligned segments drawn from Swiss official sources and your own resources, outlines the term in both languages and cites his domain and source for each case. FAQs about fast Lookup CHnell

What is a bilingual arrangement?

A tool that displays a segment side by side in the source language and its translation as published. CHnell follows the term searched for in both languages and indicates, for each segment, its domain and source, for example fedlex.admin.ch or the Federal Gazette. You see how a term is actually translated into official texts, not just how it could be translated.

Which sources feed CHnell?

Swiss Official texts: Fedlex (fedlex.admin.ch), the Federal Gazette (Bundesblatt), publications from federal administrations such as the FTA (estv.admin.ch) and fedpol (fedpol.admin.ch), which are collected in Neur.on datasets. In addition, your organization's own resources are differentiated from shared resources.

Which areas are covered?

Thirty areas, including Banking &amp; Finance, Supervisory (FINMA), Fund Prospectuses, Company Law, Articles of Association, M &amp; A, Accounting, contracts &amp; GTCS, data Protection, real estate, Criminal Law, litigation &amp; enforcement, Arbitration, Employment &amp; Social INS., tax &amp; Customs, Administrative Law, Intellectual Property, Med &amp; Pharma, Environment &amp; Trade. Each segment has its own domain and the Domains filter combines several.

Which search providers are available?

Three operators, recalled under the field of research: the quotation marks “”, the sign - and gold. They are used as in common search engines: exact expression, exclusion of a word, alternative between two words. Searches for a word, expression, or term and launches with the Search button.

Can I add my own resources?

In the Resource Type filter, the application distinguishes between old resources and shared resources: your organization's resources are searched separately from shared datasets. The deployment of your resources is being demonstrated by a Neur.on expert.

How to use CHnell without leaving a translation?

In text Translation and document, highlight a word in the source text: a fast Lookup button appears and opens CHnell on this word, in context, with the aligned segments, domain, and source of each occurrence. You will then return to your translation.
