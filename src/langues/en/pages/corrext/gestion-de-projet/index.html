What Translation projects does, exactly

Translation projects is the three-step ordering process in Corrext: Set-up, File overview, Quotes. You name the project and set the confidentiality mode, and each uploaded file is analysed (standard pages, language, legal category). The tool then offers an internal review by your team, or an external review by a legal translator or a certified agency, with instant delivery time and quote.

Everything below was observed in the application.

For instant translation without review: Text and document translation Where your data is stored and processed
Set-up

Project name generated by default (date, organisation, user) or entered freely, confidentiality mode, then target languages chosen from a checkbox list with search.The project is created as soon as you move to the next step.

Confidentiality

Standard confidentiality: processing in Switzerland or abroad depending on the engine chosen, project stored on Corrext's Swiss servers. Highly sensitive content: storage and processing in Switzerland only, for DE, FR, IT and EN.In Highly sensitive mode, translated content feeds no translation memory.

Target languages

One or more target languages per project, from the 30 languages on the platform. Each language selected appears as a chip and can be removed with one click. Next stays inactive until a language is chosen.

File overview

Formats .pdf, .doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx, .ppt, .pptx, 50 MB per file; the application recommends the original format (.docx or .xlsx rather than .pdf). For each file: number of standard pages, detected language and detected legal category, both editable.Example observed: 2.5 standard pages, French, Private Insurance.

Internal review

Your team reviews the machine translation itself. You enter the number of standard pages, the price per page and the approximate hourly rate of your review so that it is costed in the quote.Delivery time shown for the 2.5 standard page example: 1 to 2 hours.

Custom workflow

Due date (weekends greyed out), a contact link for faster delivery, priority order between files, then for each file the choice between internal or external review and the review level. The button becomes Generate quote.

The three levels

Light review, by a legal translator: significant errors, omissions and mistranslations corrected, for understanding and internal sharing. Full review, by a legal translator: consistency, idiomatic expressions, style and tone, ready for third parties. Double review & certified translation, by a specialised agency: two legal translators and a signed certificate, for legal use, publication or the authorities.

Quote and delivery time

Calculated instantly from the standard pages, the detected category, the review level and the due date: preparation, internal review, external review and total delivery time, before you order anything.Example observed: Full review, due date in ten days, 2.5 standard pages: 1 day.

Translation memory

Projects in Standard confidentiality are stored on Corrext's Swiss servers and can be reused only by you as a translation memory for your future projects. Nothing is reused in Highly sensitive mode.

Standard or Highly sensitive: what does the confidentiality mode change?

You choose when you create each project, before uploading the first file. In both cases, the project is stored on Corrext's Swiss servers. The difference lies in where processing takes place and in the translation memory. In Standard confidentiality, processing follows the engine chosen, Swiss or foreign, and the project enriches your private memory. In Highly sensitive content mode, everything stays in Switzerland and nothing is reused.

Security and data sovereignty
Swiss storage in both cases. The project is stored on Corrext's Swiss servers, whichever mode you choose.
Processing depends on the mode. Standard: in Switzerland or abroad, depending on the translation engine chosen. Highly sensitive: in Switzerland only, for DE, FR, IT and EN.
Translation memory. Standard: the project can be reused only by you as a memory for your future projects. Highly sensitive: no reuse.

Frequently asked questions about Translation projects

What is the difference between Standard confidentiality and Highly sensitive content?

In Standard confidentiality, the project is stored on Corrext's Swiss servers and the data is processed in Switzerland or abroad, depending on the translation engine chosen. The project can then serve as a translation memory, for you alone. In Highly sensitive content mode, storage and processing take place in Switzerland only, for German, French, Italian and English, and the translated content is never reused in memory.

What is a standard page?

It is the unit of measurement that Corrext shows for each file as soon as it is uploaded, next to the detected language and legal category. The estimated delivery time and the quote are based on this number of standard pages. In the example on this page, an insurance contract of a few pages counts as 2.5 standard pages.

What are the three levels of external review?

Light review, by a legal translator: significant errors, omissions and mistranslations in the machine translation are corrected, for a complete and faithful translation intended for understanding and internal sharing. Full review, by a legal translator: the translation is also adapted for consistency, idiomatic expressions, style and tone, ready to share with third parties. Double review & certified translation, by a specialised agency: full review by two qualified legal translators and a signed certificate attesting to the accuracy and impartiality of the translation, for legal use, publication or submission to the authorities.

Who reviews my documents?

You decide, file by file. With Internal review, it is your own team: you enter its price per standard page and its approximate hourly rate, so that this work is costed in the quote. With external review, it is a legal translator for Light review and Full review, or a specialised translation agency, with two translators and a signed certificate, for Double review & certified translation.

Are the quote and delivery time really instant?

Yes. As soon as the due date and review level are chosen, the Estimated time box shows the preparation, internal review, external review and total delivery time, without waiting for an agency to reply. The Generate quote button then produces the quote for these choices.

Do my projects feed a translation memory?

Only in Standard confidentiality: the project is stored on Corrext's Swiss servers and can be used only by you as a translation memory for your future projects. In Highly sensitive content mode, the translated content is never reused.
