More answers in the Help Centre and in our resources: glossary, guides and studies.

How does Neur.on ensure accuracy on Swiss law?

LexMachina, our engine, is trained on over 15 million Swiss legal and financial segments: BGE/ATF, FINMA texts, federal codes (CO, CC, DEBA). You can compare up to four alternatives from other engines, check each term in its official context with CHnell, and request a review by a lawyer-linguist.

Does my data stay in Switzerland?

You choose, project by project. In Standard mode, your data is stored on Corrext's Swiss servers and processed by the engine you select: LexMachina in Switzerland, or DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT or Claude abroad, with a pseudonymisation warning. In Highly sensitive mode, storage and processing stay in Switzerland only, with no reuse in translation memory. All of it is ISO 27001-certified and compliant with the revised FADP and the GDPR.

Which languages and domains are covered?

Thirty languages on the platform, with German, French, Italian and English native (Highly sensitive mode and LexMachina). CHnell covers 30 domains, from banking law and FINMA supervision to arbitration, tax, intellectual property and company law. For certified, notarised or apostilled translations, Corrext offers a dedicated online service.

How is it different from DeepL or a general-purpose LLM?

Generic tools translate text without knowing Swiss law or its official sources, and they host your data outside Switzerland. Corrext lets you use them where it makes sense, within a governed framework. Alongside them, it gives you an engine trained on Swiss law, comparison of alternatives, terminology checks in context and a 100% Swiss mode.

Is the platform AI model-agnostic?

Yes. In Corrext, you choose the engine for each translation: LexMachina, DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT or Claude. You compare up to four alternatives, each labelled with its engine. A long-term solution: it evolves with the state of the art and does not lock you in.

How do we integrate Neur.on with our existing tools?

Through the Microsoft Word plug-in, the Corrext web application, or the REST API for your DMS, intranets and workflows. Rights management is granular, down to document level (SharePoint / AD). On-premises deployment is possible. Book a demo to plan the integration with an expert.
