Before completing the form

What happens after I ask?

A first framing exchange: your languages, types of documents, confidentiality requirements and how your translations circulate today. Then comes a demonstration of Corrext built on this frame, then, if the tool corresponds, a framed test with a defined perimeter, users and duration.

Is demo carried out on our own documents?

Yes, that is the principle. You provide documents that are representative of your practice and we process them in Corrext during the session, in your language pairs and with your terminology. For sensitive parts, demonstration is carried out in Highly sensitive content mode: storage and treatment exclusively in Switzerland. A confidentiality agreement may be signed before the meeting.

Is there a free self-service trial?

No, and this is an assumed choice. Corrext deals with documents covered by professional or banking secrecy: access rights, engine policy, terminology and memories are configured with you. A self-service registration form does not permit this setting or responsibility. The test exists, but it is framed and follows the demonstration.

In which languages can we exchange information?

In French, German, Italian and English, both in writing and at the meeting. These are the four native languages of LexMachina and those of Swiss legal practice.

A question before writing to us?

If your topic doesn't appear on a form, a message is sufficient. We answer in French, German, Italian or English.
