An example of an article of the Banking Act

A sentence of banking law, translated from French to German in Corrext. The two exits below were collected on the same day, in the same alternative panel.

Source · French

The supervised institution shall ensure compliance with banking secrecy in accordance with Article 47 BankA and implement FINMA's anti-money laundering requirements.

LexMachina

DAS beaufsichtigte Institut sorgt für die Einhaltung des Bankgeheimnisses nach Artikel 47 BankG und setzt die Anforderungen der FINMA zur Bekämpfung der Geldwäscherei um.

DeepL Pro

DAS unterstellte Institut sorgt für die Einhaltung des Bankgeheimnisses im Sinne von Art. 47 BankG und setzt die Anforderungen der FINMA zur Bekämpfung der Geldwäscherei um.

Both withdrawals are fair: each quotes Art. 47 BankG, the German abbreviation of the Banking Act, and uses the Swiss term Geldwäscherei rather than Geldwäsche. They differ on one point that a legal translator will decide against the applicable German text, not on the ear: the way in which the “supervised institution”, on the one hand, is handled, on the other hand, is unterstellt.

Actual exits obtained in Corrext, on this precise phrase, in French to German, in the alternatives panel. This is an isolated and dated example, not a measure: another sentence, another pair of languages or another generation would give another result. That's why Corrext displays the alternatives to their engines and checks each term in CHnell before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions about DeepL and legal translation

Can I use DeepL for a document covered by professional secrecy?

The question is not the quality of the engine, it is the transfer of the document out of Switzerland. In Corrext, choosing DeepL Pro triggers the Attorney-Client privilege opinion: the organization requires that the document be entirely pseudonymised, and prohibits such engines for sensitive files. For a non-pseudonymised document, choose LexMachina or the “high sensitive content” method, which requires treatment exclusively in Switzerland. The rule that binds you remains that of your firm and your supervisor.

Does DeepL process my data in Switzerland?

No. DeepL is a German publisher and its infrastructure is located outside of Switzerland. In Corrext, DeepL Pro is therefore classified as a third-party engine hosted abroad and triggers the pseudonymisation notice. For storage and treatment exclusively in Switzerland, the Highly sensitive content, available in German, French, Italian and English, must be LexMachina selected or activated.

does Corrext replace DeepL?

No: Corrext can use DeepL Pro. The engine is chosen on each consignment, together with LexMachina, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude, and the alternative panel compares up to four engine proposals. What Corrext adds is no additional driver: this is the framework around Swiss storage, pre-mailing notice, CHnell concordance, human review ordered in the tool, quotation and certification.

What does a motivated motor mean for Swiss law?

LexMachina is trained in over 15 million Swiss multilingual legal and financial segments. Specifically, it is familiar with the abbreviations and conventions of the country: CO and GOLD, LB and BankG, PILA and IPRG, the Swiss graph in ss, the Federal Supreme Court and not a foreign jurisdiction. This does not make him infallible: no engine is a source of right, and that is why Corrext displays the alternatives and the concordance.

Does DeepL Pro retain my texts?

DeepL publicly States that texts translated by its Pro clients are deleted after translation and are not used to train its models: this is a serious commitment, and we have no reason to doubt it. The point that remains open for a file covered by professional secrecy is not the retention, it is the transfer of the document out of Switzerland and the absence of any warning at the time of dispatch.

How can a Swiss legal term be checked after a translation?

In Corrext, follow the word in the source text and click fast lookup: the CHnell concordance shows aligned segments drawn from official Swiss sources, with the domain and source of each occurrence, and a Show in context link. Verzugszins returns over 300 results and Bankgeheimnis returns over 400. It is this traceability that a translation engine alone does not provide.
