What Text and document translation does, exactly

Text and document translation is the instant translation tool in Corrext. It translates a text or a batch of files with the engine of your choice and compares alternatives, each labelled with its engine. It checks a term in its official context via CHnell and rephrases a text in five styles. Everything below was observed in the application.

For human review: Translation projects Where your data is processed
Text translation

Up to 10,000 characters, source language detected automatically, translation as you type, with no button to press. Copy, clear, undo and redo buttons.Demo outputs: Art. 104 CO, Art. 47 BankA, Art. 186 PILA, as produced by LexMachina.

File translation

Up to 25 files per upload, 50 MB per file: .pdf, .doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx, .ppt, .pptx. The translation keeps the original format.Tip from the application: upload the original format (.docx or .xlsx rather than .pdf) when you have it.

PDF to Word

Converts a PDF, including a scanned one, into an editable Word document, before translation or for internal work.The Highly sensitive content switch is greyed out on this tab: it does not translate.

Engines

LexMachina (Swiss, default), DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude, chosen for each upload. Engines hosted abroad trigger your organisation's Attorney-Client privilege notice.

Alternatives

The spark button opens up to four suggestions, each labelled with its engine, with a pager and Generate more. You compare before you settle on a wording.

Fast Lookup

A word highlighted in the source opens CHnell on that word, in context: aligned segments, domain, official source, Show in context.Verzugszins: over 300 results; Bankgeheimnis: over 400 results.

Rephrasing

Rephrases a text of up to 5,000 characters. Styles Formal legal, Financial, Simplified, Formal, Informal; Shorten option. Changed words are underlined.

Languages

30 languages on the platform, including Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and the European languages. German, French, Italian and English native with LexMachina and in Highly sensitive mode.

Confidentiality

Storage on Corrext's Swiss servers in all cases. Processing in Switzerland only with LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode.

What happens when you choose an engine hosted abroad?

Corrext does not prohibit it: it applies your organisation's policy. As soon as DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT or Claude is selected, the Attorney-Client privilege notice reminds you that only fully pseudonymised documents may be entrusted to them, and that sensitive matters are off limits. With LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode, everything stays in Switzerland.

Frequently asked questions about Text and document translation

Which file formats does Text and document translation accept?

The .pdf, .doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx, .ppt and .pptx formats, up to 25 files of 50 MB each per upload. The translation is delivered in the original format. The PDF to Word tab converts a PDF, including a scanned one, into a Word document.

How much text can I translate at once?

Up to 10,000 characters in Text translation and 5,000 characters in Rephrasing. For longer texts, upload the document in File translation or create a translation project.

Which translation engines are available?

LexMachina, the Swiss engine trained on Swiss law, as well as DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude. You choose the engine for each upload, and the Alternatives panel compares up to four suggestions, each labelled with its engine.

What does the Highly sensitive content switch do?

It enforces storage and processing in Switzerland only, for German, French, Italian and English. According to the definition Corrext shows in Translation projects, the content is not reused in a translation memory. The switch is greyed out on the PDF to Word tab, which does not translate.

How do I check a term during a translation?

Highlight the word in the source text and click Fast Lookup: CHnell shows aligned segments from official Swiss sources, with the domain and source of each occurrence.

Does Text and document translation remove the need for review by a legal translator?

No. Text and document translation is for understanding, preparing and working fast. For a document intended for third parties, for publication or for an authority, create a translation project with Light review, Full review or Double review & certified translation.
