Frequently asked questions about Security and sovereignty

Where is my data hosted?

On Swiss servers. Corrext relies on the infrastructure of INFOMANIAK, in Switzerland. Projects and files you file are stored there, regardless of the confidentiality method and translation engine you choose.

Is my data processed in Switzerland?

Storage is in any case. Processing is only done when Highly sensitive content is enabled, or when you translate with LexMachina. In Standard mode with a third-party engine, the text is processed by an engine hosted abroad: the application notifies you before sending it.

Can I use DeepL or ChatGPT for a file covered by professional secrecy?

Corrext does not technically prohibit you from doing so: it applies your organization's policy when you choose the engine. The Attorney-Client privilege Opinion then appears and recalls that only fully pseudonymized documents may be entrusted to an engine hosted abroad, and that their use is strictly prohibited for sensitive files or files of financial importance. For a room covered by professional secrecy, the choice is LexMachina or the Highly sensitive content.

Do my documents serve to train a role model?

No. LexMachina is trained on public Swiss legal and financial bodies, not on your files. In Standard mode, your projects feed into a translation memory that can be used exclusively by you as defined by Corrext. in Highly sensitive content mode, no memory is supplied.

What becomes of a highly sensitive project?

It is stored and processed exclusively in Switzerland, and its translated content is not reused in translation memories for the following projects. The fashion is available for English, French, Italian and German. It is settled by a switch in text translation and document and selects to create a draft translation.

is Neur.on subject to the CLOUD act?

No. Neur.on AI Solutions SA is a Swiss company based in Fribourg, and the data is hosted in Switzerland on a Swiss infrastructure: the US CLOUD act does not apply to suppliers subject to US jurisdiction. If you choose a third-party engine that is hosted abroad, the text that you send to it is subject to the law applicable to that provider.

Can we deploy to our own servers?

Yes. Corrext can be deployed on-premises, in your own environment, when your security policy or regulator so requires. A REST API also calls the translation from your DMS, intranet or existing workflows. The choice depends on your infrastructure and your obligations: this is a discussion to have during the demo.
