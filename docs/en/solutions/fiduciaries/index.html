Which level of certification should you request?

As a general rule, certification by the provider is enough for internal use or for sending to a private third party. A Swiss authority or court often asks for notarised certification. A foreign authority in a state party to the Hague Convention will in principle require an apostille. The receiving authority alone decides.

The three levels of certification
Corrext certification. A certificate signed and stamped by the provider, for everyday use.
Notarised certification. A Swiss notary is involved, which Swiss authorities often require.
Notarised and apostilled. An apostille from the cantonal State Chancellery, for states party to the Hague Convention.

What Corrext changes for a fiduciary firm

Translating a company document is rarely a literary matter. It is a matter of acceptance. The recipient, whether a bank, an authority or a foreign partner, accepts or rejects the document. So Corrext organises the work around the level of certification required.

Frequently asked questions from fiduciary firms

How do I order a certified translation of a commercial register extract?

The online service takes four steps: search for the company by name or UID number, choose the certification level, choose the delivery method, choose the delivery time. Everything is done online, with no appointment.

What is the difference between certification, notarisation and an apostille?

Certification is the provider's attestation that the translation is accurate. Notarisation adds the involvement of a Swiss notary. The apostille, issued by the cantonal State Chancellery under the Hague Convention of 5 October 1961, authenticates the notary's signature for states party to the Convention.

Into which languages are extracts translated?

The service lists more than 30 target languages, including German, French, Italian, English and Spanish.

Who translates commercial register extracts?

Legal translators who specialise in corporate housekeeping, assisted by models trained on Swiss commercial register extracts.

What about other company documents?

Articles of association, minutes, reports and appendices go through Translation projects, with the review level suited to the recipient, up to Double review & certified translation by a specialist agency.

Do client files remain confidential?

Storage is Swiss in every case, and Highly sensitive content mode also keeps processing in Switzerland, with no reuse of the content as translation memory.
