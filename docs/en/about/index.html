“Technology Empowers, Not Overpowers”

Our guiding principle fits in one sentence, and it shapes everything else. A legal translation carries responsibility: that of the legal expert who signs it, not that of a model. So we build tools that augment legal professionals. They give them the context, the official terminology and the alternatives they need to decide quickly, and they leave the decision to them. An engine that decided in their place would not be progress: it would be a transfer of risk.

That is why we never predict the end of the translator or the lawyer-linguist. The four quality levels below put this principle into practice: you choose the share of machine and the share of human work that the stakes justify, and you always know which one you chose.

See the Corrext platform
The AI proposes, the legal expert decides. Alternatives compared, terminology verifiable in context, harmonisation suggested: never an automatic approval.
The official source is shown. CHnell displays where a term comes from, in which field and in which text, so that the choice can be justified.
Control stays with your organisation. Engine policy, access rights, Highly sensitive content mode: the rules are yours, and the tool applies them.

Four quality levels, because not every document carries the same stakes

You choose the human share document by document. It is the most concrete form our principle takes.

LEVEL 1

Machine translation

For quick understanding: document review, internal reading, sorting a case file. The engine alone, without review, and you know it.

LEVEL 2

Post-editing

Light or Full review: contracts, General Terms and Conditions, correspondence. A lawyer-linguist reviews the text so that it can be published.

LEVEL 3

Legal review

For high stakes: legal opinions, publications, binding documents. The review is carried out by experts who take responsibility for it.

LEVEL 4

Certification and apostille

Certified and legalised translation, through a network of expert translators, for official files.

What is Swiss, exactly

“Swiss solution” is a phrase many providers use. Here is what it covers in our case, point by point, so that you can compare it with any other offer.

The Corrext platform Request a demo
The entity

Neur.on AI Solutions SA, a company under Swiss law based at Place de la Gare 15, 1700 Fribourg. No parent company outside Switzerland holds our clients' data.

The infrastructure

Hosting with Infomaniak, in Switzerland. Your documents are stored on Swiss servers in every case, beyond the reach of the US CLOUD Act, in line with the revised FADP and the GDPR.ISO 27001 certification obtained in February 2024.

The engine

LexMachina is trained on Swiss legal and financial data: Federal Supreme Court decisions (BGE/ATF), texts of the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority FINMA, codes. Over 15 million multilingual segments, not a general-purpose model with a glossary added on top.

The processing

Exclusively in Switzerland with LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode. Third-party engines, hosted abroad, remain available, but they trigger the pseudonymisation notice defined by your organisation.

Quadrilingualism

Native German, French, Italian and English in every pair, and 30 languages on the platform. Federal law is authoritative in German, French and Italian, and international matters are handled in English: a Swiss legal tool cannot afford to treat any of these languages as secondary.

The research

An innovation project supported by Innosuisse, carried out with the iCoSys institute of HEIA-FR. Applied research stays in Switzerland too.

The terminology

CHnell indexes official Swiss sources across 30 domains, from legislation to financial publications. It also takes in your own resources, without mixing them with those of another client.

An expert team in Swiss LegalTech

Neur.on brings together lawyer-linguists, natural language processing engineers and data scientists in Fribourg. Some know what a term commits you to in court. Others know how to teach it to a machine.
