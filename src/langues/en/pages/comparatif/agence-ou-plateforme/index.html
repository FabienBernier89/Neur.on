External Replay, as ordered in the tool

In the Project Tunnel, each file receives its language, its internal or external mode of replay, and its level. These three levels are those of the application, faithfully returned.

Light Review legal translator

The translator corrects significant errors, omissions and contradictions of translation. The result is a complete and semantically accurate translation, recommended to understand a document and transmit it internally.

Full review legal translator

The translator reproduces, adapts and modifies the automatic translation, paying particular attention to consistency, idiomatism, style and tone. This is the correct legal translation of your documents, ready to be shared with third parties.

Double review and certified translation

Complete review by two qualified legal translators. A certificate signed by the specialised agency responsible for the project confirms the accuracy and impartiality of the translation. This is the level appropriate for judicial use, publication or transmission to an authority.

Definitions displayed per Corrext in quotes. The same project can mix internal and external review, file by file. Internal review is valued with your own standard page rate, entered by you in the tool.

Why we are not trying to eliminate agencies

Because human reinterpretation remains the only way to commit someone to a text, and because certification requires signature. What we are disputing is not the work of legal translators: it is the fact that an order is placed to simply read a document, while eighty percent of the documents in a file are never intended to leave the practice. Corrext sorts: the machine to understand, the human to commit, and the choice is made by file, with its display time.

Frequently asked questions about agencies and platforms

does Corrext replace my translation agency?

No. Corrext commands external review: in the project tunnel, a document may be sent to a legal translator or to a certified agency, in light review, full review or double review with certification. What the platform removes is not the agency, it is the obligation to place an order simply to read a part. Human work focuses where it relies, on the documents that are binding.

Who links the documents to Corrext?

Two ports, selected file by file. Internal review: your own lawyers or translators reappear on the platform, and the estimated time appears before you launch. External Review: the review is assigned to a legal translator or a certified agency, with an estimated time frame and an expiry date selected in a schedule. The same project can mix the two, row by row, in the file table.

What is the difference between the light review, full review and double review?

Light Review: a translator corrects significant errors, omissions and contradictions; the result is complete and accurate, recommended to understand and transmit internally. Full Review: the translator also adapts consistency, idiomatism, style and tone; this is the ready-to-share version with third parties. Double review and certified translation: complete review by two qualified legal translators, with a certificate signed by the specialized agency certifying accuracy and impartiality, for judicial use, publication or authority.

How can I get an estimate and a deadline?

They are calculated in the tool at the quotes stage of the Project Tunnel. Corrext counts the standard pages of each file, detects the language and legal category of the document, and then displays the preparation time, internal review time and external review time, as well as the total cost before taxes. You adjust the review level and the due date, and the estimate follows. No prior exchange is required to obtain this estimate.

Can I keep the agency I already work with?

External review ordered in Corrext is conducted through our network of certified legal translators and agencies. However, nothing prevents you from continuing to entrust your usual service provider with the documents that fall under his responsibility: your teams translate and replay the document in Corrext, and export it to its original format. If you want to integrate your own service provider into the project tunnel, this is something to consider together in a demo.
