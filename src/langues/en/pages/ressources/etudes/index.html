What we prohibit ourselves from doing

Three rules that explain why you will not find spectacular rankings here.

No score without published corpus

A percentage without a corpus, date and protocol means nothing. Until the corpus is described, no numbers are published.

No hidden comparison

The engines under comparison are named, the versions dated, and the faulty outflows of our own engine are listed in the same way as the others.

No raw speed measurements

The speed depends on the load and the network. It says nothing about the correctness of a legal reference, the only criterion that relies on a binding document.
