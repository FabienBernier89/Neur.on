What project management does exactly

Project management is the three-step Corrext control tunnel: set-up, queue overview, quotes. It names the project, sets the confidentiality method, analyzes each file filed (standard pages, language, legal category), and then offers internal review by your team or external review by a legal translator or a certified agency, with instant delay and quotations.

All of the following has been observed in the application.

For snapshot without preview: text translation and document Where your data is stored and processed
set-up

The name of the project generated by default (date, organization, user) or entered freely, confidentiality mode, then target languages selected from a lookup checklist. the project is created as soon as it moves to the next step.

confidentiality

Standard confidentiality: processing in Switzerland or abroad according to the selected engine, registered on the Swiss servers of Corrext. Highly sensitive content: Storage and processing exclusively in Switzerland, for EN, FR, IT and DE. in sensitive mode, translated content does not feed any translation memory.

target languages

One or more target languages per project, from the 30 languages of the platform. Each selected language displays in a chip and pulls down with one click; next remains inactive as long as none is selected.

overview file

. PDF,. doc,. docx,. xls,. xlsx,. ppt,. pptx, 50 MB per file; the application advises the original format (. docx or. xlsx rather than. PDF). For each file: number of standard pages, language detected, and legal category detected, both editable. example observed: 2.5 Standard pages, French, private Insurance.

Internal Review

your team connects the AutoTranslation itself. You specify the number of standard pages, the price per page, and the approximate hourly price of your review to value it in quotation. time lag displayed for example 2.5 standard pages: 1-2 hours.

Custom workflow

Expiration date (grey weekends), contact link for faster delivery, priority order between files, and for each file the choice between internal or external review and the Replay level. The button becomes Generate quote.

three levels

light Review, by a legal translator: material errors, omissions and corrected countermeasures, to understand and forward internally. full review, by a legal translator: consistency, idiomatic looks, style and tone, ready for others. Double review and certified translation, by a specialized agency: two legal translators and signed certificate for legal use, publication or the authorities.

quotations and time period

calculated instantaneously from standard pages, detected category, review level, and expiry date: preparation, internal review, external review and total delay, before any command. example observed: Full review, 10-day maturity, 2.5 standard pages: 1 day.

Translation Memory

Standard confidentiality projects are registered on Swiss Corrext servers and reusable exclusively by you as a translation memory for your future projects. Nothing is re-used in Highly sensitive mode.

Standard or highly sensitive: What does the confidentiality method change?

The choice is made when each project is created, before the first file is filed. In both cases, the project is registered on the Swiss servers of Corrext. The difference relates to the place of processing and the translation memory: under Standard confidentiality, the processing follows the chosen Swiss or foreign engine and the project enriches your private memory; in Highly sensitive content, everything stays in Switzerland and nothing is reused.

FAQs on Project Management

What is the difference between Standard confidentiality and Highly sensitive content?

In Standard confidentiality, the project is registered on Swiss Corrext servers and the data is processed in Switzerland or abroad according to the chosen translation engine; the project can then be used by you as a translation memory. In Highly sensitive content, storage and processing are exclusively Swiss, for English, French, Italian and German, and translated content is never reused in memory.

What is a standard page?

It is the counting unit that Corrext displays for each file as soon as it is filed, next to the language and legal category detected. The estimated time and cost estimate are based on this standard number of pages. In the example on this page, an insurance contract with a few pages has 2.5 standard pages.

What are the three levels of external review?

Light Review, by a legal translator: material errors, omissions and contradictions of translation shall be corrected for a full and faithful translation intended for internal understanding and transmission. Full review by a legal translator: the translation is also suitable for consistency, idiomatic language, style and tone, ready to be shared with third parties. Double review and certified translation by a specialized agency: complete review by two qualified legal translators and signed certificate attesting to the accuracy and impartiality of the translation, for legal use, publication or delivery to the authorities.

Who links my documents?

You decide it file by file. In Internal Review, it is your own team: you provide its price per standard page and its approximate hourly price to value this work in the cost estimate. In external replay, it is a legal translator for the light review and full review, or a specialized translation agency, with two translators and a signed certificate, for the double review and certified translation.

Are the quotation and time period really instantaneous?

Yes. As soon as the expiry date and the review level are chosen, the Estimated time box displays the preparation, internal review, external review and total delay, without waiting for an agency to return. The Generate quote button then produces the cost estimate for these choices.

Do my projects feed into a translation memory?

Only in Standard confidentiality: the project is registered on Corrext's Swiss servers and can be used exclusively by you as a translation memory for your future projects. In Highly sensitive content, translated content is never used again.
