Before you fill in the form

What happens after I send my request?

First, a scoping discussion: your languages, your document types, your confidentiality constraints and the way your translations circulate today. Next comes a demonstration of Corrext built on that scoping. Then, if the tool is a good fit, a supervised trial with a scope, users and a duration defined together.

Can the demo use our own documents?

Yes, that is the principle. You bring documents representative of your practice, and we process them in Corrext during the session, in your language pairs and with your terminology. For sensitive documents, the demonstration runs in Highly sensitive content mode: storage and processing exclusively in Switzerland. A non-disclosure agreement can be signed before the session.

Is there a self-service free trial?

No, and that is a deliberate choice. Corrext handles documents covered by professional secrecy or banking secrecy: access rights, engine policy, terminology and translation memories are configured with you. A self-service sign-up form allows neither this scoping nor this accountability. A trial is available, but it is supervised and it follows the demonstration.

Which languages can we use with you?

German, French, Italian and English, in writing and in meetings. These are the four native languages of LexMachina and the languages of Swiss legal practice.

A question before you write to us?

If your request does not fit into a form, a message is enough. We reply in German, French, Italian or English.
