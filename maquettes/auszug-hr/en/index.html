Covers all certification needs

Corrext offers three levels of certification to match every legal and administrative requirement:

  • Standard Corrext Certification: The translation is issued with a certificate, duly signed and stamped, confirming the translator’s credentials and certifying that the translation has been prepared with due care and to the best of the translator’s ability.

  • Notarized Certification: For documents that must carry additional legal weight, the Corrext certificate can be notarized by a Swiss notary public. This ensures formal recognition by courts and administrative bodies.

  • Notarized & Apostilled Certification: When documents are destined for use abroad, we can provide an apostille from the competent Swiss State Chancellery pursuant to the Hague Convention of 5 October 1961. This formality allows recognition by foreign courts and authorities in countries that are parties to the Convention.

Standard Corrext Certification

The translation is accompanied by a duly signed and stamped certificate attesting to the translator’s credentials and certifying that, to the best of the translator’s knowledge, skill, and belief, the translation is complete and accurate.

Notarized Certification

The signature on the Corrext Translation Certificate is duly notarized by a Swiss notary public. This additional formality may be requested by courts or administrative authorities to validate the translation for official use.

Notarized & Apostilled Certification

To facilitate use abroad, the notarization can be authenticated by an “apostille” issued by the competent Swiss State Chancellery in accordance with the Hague Convention of 5 October 1961. This additional formality may be requested by foreign courts or administrative authorities. The apostille is accepted exclusively in countries that are parties to the Hague Convention.
