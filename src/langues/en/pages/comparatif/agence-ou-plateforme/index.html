The short answer

A translation agency brings contractual liability and a human contact; a generic tool brings immediacy. Corrext replaces neither. It translates straight away, then orders external review from a legal translator or a certified agency, only for the documents that require it.

This page compares three ways of organising the work, not three named providers, and does not discuss prices.

External review, as you order it in the tool

In the project workflow, each file is given its language, its review mode (internal or external) and its level. These three levels are the application's own, described faithfully.

Light reviewLegal translator

The translator corrects significant errors, omissions and mistranslations in the machine translation. The result is a complete and semantically accurate translation, recommended for understanding a document and circulating it internally.

Full reviewLegal translator

The translator reviews, adapts and modifies the machine translation, with particular attention to consistency, idiomatic wording, style and tone. The result is a sound legal translation of your documents, ready to be shared with third parties.

Double review & certified translation

A full review by two qualified legal translators. A certificate signed by the specialised agency in charge of the project attests to the accuracy and impartiality of the translation. This is the right level for use in court, publication or submission to an authority.

Definitions displayed by Corrext at the Quotes step. A single project can mix internal and external review, file by file. Internal review is costed at your own standard page rate, which you enter in the tool.

Why we are not trying to do away with agencies

Because human review remains the only way to make someone answerable for a text, and because certification requires a signature. What we challenge is not the work of legal translators. It is having to place an order just to read a document, when most of the documents in a case file are never meant to leave the firm. Corrext sorts: machine translation for understanding, human review for anything binding, and you choose file by file, with the delivery time shown.

Frequently asked questions about agencies and platforms

Does Corrext replace my translation agency?

No. Corrext is where you order external review: in the project workflow, a document can be assigned to a legal translator or a certified agency, in Light review, Full review or Double review & certified translation. What the platform removes is not the agency, but the need to place an order just to read a document. Human work is concentrated where it counts, on binding documents.

Who reviews documents in Corrext?

There are two routes, chosen file by file. Internal review: your own legal experts or translators review in the platform, and the estimated time is shown before you start. External review: the review is assigned to a legal translator or a certified agency, with an estimated delivery time and a due date chosen in a calendar. A single project can mix both, row by row, in the file table.

What is the difference between Light review, Full review and Double review?

Light review: a translator corrects significant errors, omissions and mistranslations; the result is complete and accurate, recommended for understanding and internal circulation. Full review: the translator also works on consistency, idiomatic wording, style and tone; this is the version ready to be shared with third parties. Double review & certified translation: a full review by two qualified legal translators, with a certificate signed by the specialised agency attesting to accuracy and impartiality, for use in court, publication or submission to an authority.

How do I get a quote and a delivery time?

They are calculated in the tool, at the Quotes step of the project workflow. Corrext counts the standard pages of each file and detects the language and legal category of the document. It then shows the preparation time, the internal review time and the external review time, together with the total cost before tax. You adjust the review level and the due date, and the quote follows. No prior exchange is needed to get this estimate.

Can I keep the agency I already work with?

External review ordered in Corrext goes through our network of legal translators and certified agencies. Nothing stops you, however, from continuing to send your usual provider the documents that fall within its remit: your teams translate and review in Corrext, then export the document in its original format. If you would like to bring your own provider into the project workflow, we can look at this together in a demo.
