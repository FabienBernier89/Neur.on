Frequently asked questions about the API and on-premises deployment

Do you offer an API to integrate translation into our tools?

Yes. A REST API lets you call the engine from your own systems: document management, intranet, business workflows. We do not publish open technical documentation. The interface contract, authentication and scope of calls are set during scoping, with your IT department and our technical team.

Can we deploy the solution in our own infrastructure?

On-premises deployment is possible for the most sensitive environments, that is, when your internal rules require that nothing leaves your network. It is a full integration project, scoped with your IT department: architecture, sizing, updates and operation are defined before any commitment.

Can our access rights be taken over from SharePoint or Active Directory?

This is provided for as part of an integration project. Access rights can be synchronised with your directory and your document spaces, so that everyone finds in Corrext the same scope they already have in your systems. This synchronisation is not a self-service setting in the web interface: it is set up with your IT department.

Can we integrate the engine into our own product, as a white label?

This is the use case of LegalTech companies and legal publishers: translation becomes a feature of your product, called via API, without your users changing tools. The functional scope, the brand displayed and the terms are set out in the contract during scoping.

Where is data processed during an API call?

The same rules apply as in the interface: storage takes place on Swiss servers in all cases. Processing takes place in Switzerland only with LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode, available for German, French, Italian and English. The third-party engines, DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude, are hosted abroad and trigger the pseudonymisation notice defined by your organisation. With an on-premises deployment, processing stays within your own infrastructure.
