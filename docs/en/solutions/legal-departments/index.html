Frequently asked questions from legal departments

How is the quote calculated?

From the number of standard pages detected in each file, the legal category recognised, the review level chosen and the delivery date. The delivery time and the cost are shown in the application before you confirm anything.

Can we review in-house rather than externally?

Yes. The Internal review option estimates your teams' review time from the number of standard pages, and you can put your own value on that time in the quote.

How are access rights managed?

Rights are set down to document level and synchronised with SharePoint and Active Directory.

Can our past translations be reused?

Projects created with Standard confidentiality can be used as translation memory, reusable by your organisation only. Projects in Highly sensitive content mode never are.

Do teams need training?

The interface follows familiar steps: upload a file, choose a language, start. The Help Centre documents every screen, and the demo is done on your own documents.

Can Corrext be integrated with our tools?

A REST API lets you connect translation to your document management systems and intranets, and deployment on your own servers is possible for the most sensitive environments.
