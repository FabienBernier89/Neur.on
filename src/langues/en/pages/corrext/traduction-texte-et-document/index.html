What text and document translation do exactly

Text Translation and document is Corrext's instant translation tool. It translates a text or file batch with the engine of your choice, compares engine alternatives, checks a term in its official context via CHnell, and reformulates a text in five styles. All of the following has been observed in the application.

For human review: project management Where your data is processed
text translation

Up to 10,000 characters, source language automatically detected, translation to entry without a button. Buttons copy, delete, cancel and re-establish. outputs of the demo: Art. 104 CO, Art. 47 BA, Art. 186 PILA, as produced by LexMachina.

file translation

Up to 25 files per submission, 50 MB per file:. PDF,. doc,. docx,. xls,. xlsx,. ppt,. pptx. Translation retains original format. App tip: file original format (. docx or. xlsx rather than. PDF) when available.

PDF to Word

Converting a PDF, , including scanned, to Editable Word document, before translation or for internal work. the Highly sensitive content switch is greased on this tab: it does not translate.

engines

LexMachina (Swiss default), DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude, selected for each shipment. Engines hosted abroad trigger the Attorney-Client privilege opinion of your organization.

alternatives

The spark button opens up to four proposals, each assigned to its engine, with a paginator and “generate more”. You compare before selecting a formulation.

fast lookup

A highlighted word in source opens CHnell on this word, in context: aligned segments, domain, official source, “Show in context”. Verzugszins: more than 300 results; Bankgeheimnis: more than 400 results.

Rephrasing

Rewriting text up to 5,000 characters. Formal legal, Financial, implified, Formal, Informal; shORTEN option. The amended words are underlined.

languages

30 languages on the platform, including Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and European languages. German, French, Italian and English native with LexMachina and Highly sensitive mode.

confidentiality

Storage on Swiss servers of Corrext in any case. treatment exclusively in Switzerland with LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode.

What happens when you choose an engine hosted abroad?

Corrext does not prohibit you: it applies the policy of your organization. As soon as DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT or Claude is selected, Attorney-Client privilege advises that only fully pseudonymized documents may be entrusted to them, and that sensitive files are prohibited. With LexMachina or Highly sensitive content, everything stays in Switzerland.

FAQs on Translation text and document

What file formats does text translation and document accept?

The. PDF,. doc,. docx,. xls,. xlsx,. ppt, and. pptx formats, up to 25 files of 50 MB each per shipment. The translation shall be delivered in the original format. The PDF to Word tab converts a PDF, including scanned, into a Word document.

How many texts can I translate at once?

Up to 10,000 characters in text translation and 5,000 characters in Rephrasing. After that, leave the document in file translation or create a draft translation.

Which translation engines are available?

LexMachina, the Swiss motor powered by Swiss law, as well as DeepL Pro, Azure OpenAI GPT and Claude. The engine selects each time it is sent, and the alternative panel compares up to four proposals, each assigned to its engine.

What does the hot sensitive content switch do?

It requires storage and treatment exclusively in Switzerland for German, French, Italian and English; as defined by Corrext in Project Management, the content is not reused in a translation memory. It is grey on the PDF to Word tab, which does not translate.

How do I check a term during a translation?

Highlight the word in the source text and click fast lookup: CHnell has aligned segments drawn from official sources in Switzerland, with the domain and source of each occurrence.

Does text translation and document exempt a legal translator from reproducing it?

No. Text translation and document are used to understand, prepare and work quickly. For a document intended for a third party, publication, or authority, create a Translation Project with light, full, or Double Review and Certified Translation.
