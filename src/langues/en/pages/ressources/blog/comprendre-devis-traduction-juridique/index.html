in brief

A legal translation quote consists of four or five lines, each covering a substantive decision. This is what every line means, what every level of review guarantees, and what a quote never says: the time you will spend as a lawyer.

Two quotations for the same contract may differ from one to three without one being abusive. They do not count the same thing, they do not promise the same work and they do not carry the same risk. To read a quotation is to know what you buy: a comprehensible text, a transferable text, or a binding text.

1. Why you don't count on paper pages

The counting unit for a translation quote is the standard page. This is not a sheet in your document: it is a conventional amount of text. A memorandum of ten widely ventilated leaves, with titles and blanks, may represent fewer standard pages than a contract of three dense leaves in body 9.

This unit exists for a simple reason: it is the only unit that remains stable when changing its format. The same content in Word, scanned PDF, or presentation gives a different number of sheets and the same standard number of pages. It is therefore on this basis that both the price and the time limit are calculated.

The difficulty is that the exact definition of the standard page varies from provider to provider, in number of characters or words, including spaces or not. This is the first thing to ask in writing, even before comparing two amounts: without this definition, the comparison makes no sense.

the helpful procedure. ask each provider, for the same file, how many standard pages they hold. If the two figures differ, you know where the difference comes from before you discuss the rest.

2. What the machine detects before encrypting

On a modern translation platform, the cost estimate is no longer produced after human reading of the document: it is calculated at the time of filing, based on what the system recognises. In Corrext, filing a file displays three information, all of which can be edited: the number of standard pages, language detected and legal category detected of the document.

The category is not an administrative detail. It determines the reference terminology, the profile of the relector to be mobilised and, consequently, the processing time. A private insurance contract, a supervisory circular and an arbitration agreement do not resemble one another at the same speed.

systematically check this detection. a poorly categorized document produces a quote just in its form and false in its merits, because it mobilises the wrong skill. This is one of the few points where thirty seconds of attention change the result.

3. The three External Replay levels, in the text

The levels of review are not quality levels in the commercial sense of the term: they are different areas of intervention. These are the exact definitions in Corrext, both in English and in the app, followed by what they mean in practice.

light Review · legal Translator

Correcting false information

“The output of the machine translation is Reviewed by a Translator to correct any significant errors, omissions or mistranslations. The result is a complete and Semantically accurate translation of the document, recommended for Understanding and Internal transmission.”

A translator uses the automatic exit to eliminate significant errors, omissions and contradictions. You get complete and accurate text on the background. This is not guaranteed: stylistic homogeneity, idiomatism, keeping a register. This is the right level to understand a file, prepare a meeting, circulate internally.

full review · legal translator

Make the text transferable

“The output of the machine translation is Reviewed by a Translator, adjusted and modified, with additional focus on consistency, idiomatic wording, style and tone. This ensures has good legal translation of your documents, ready to be shared with third parties.”

The translator goes beyond correction: he adapts, harmonizes, works on consistency, idiomatic formulation, style and tone. The difference to the previous level is not an extra elegance, it is the fact that the document can leave your organization without showing you. This is the level expected for a document addressed to an opposing party, client or partner.

Double Review & Certified Translation

Incurring liability

“Full review of the documents by two qualified legal translators. A certificate signed by the Specialized Translation Agency in charge of the Project attests to the accuracy and impartiality of the translation. Suitable for documents intended for legal use, publication, or submission to authorities.”

Two qualified legal translators reproduce in full, and one specialized agency signs a certificate attesting to the accuracy and impartiality of the translation. What is changing here is not just the number of pairs of eyes: a third confirms this in writing. This is the level required for legal use, publication or filing with an authority.

Remember the logic of progression: the first level corrects, the second harmonises, and the third confirms. Choosing the level means answering only one question: who will read this document, and what happens if it contains an error?

4. Internal review: apparently free, paid in legal hours

In addition to external replay, most organizations use internal reinterpretation: an employee from the law firm or legal department links the translation themselves. On a quotation, this line appears to be zero. This is exactly where the expenditure lies.

Corrext deals with this issue in an unusual and informative manner. In the internal review specifications, the application asks to specify the price per standard page that you will bill yourself for this review, as well as an order of time magnitude. It then shows a total cost that includes your own work. As long as you have not seized anything, this total remains at zero: the tool literally shows that internal replay appears free because nobody has valued it.

The time estimate is produced from the outset. On a 2.5-page standard document observed in the application, internal replay was estimated to be 1 to 2 hours. Report this order of magnitude to the actual volume of a folder and to the person who will link: the question is not whether external replay costs more, but whether it costs more than the hours it freezes.

to be done once and for all. set an internal replay time value for each connecting profile. Without it, any arbitration between internal and external review is intuitive, and intuition always chooses what does not appear on an invoice.

5. The time limit is not an independent variable

The time limit is derived from the same data as the price: the number of standard pages, the category of document, the level of review selected and the combination of languages. It is therefore not traded separately, and a quotation that announces a maturity without requesting this information announces an intention, not a commitment.

Two mechanisms merit attention in an online quote tunnel.

  • the expiry date is an input, not an output. You specify when you want the document; the system calculates the corresponding preview time and tells you if it will hold. In Corrext, weekends are grey in the calendar, and a link suggests contacting for faster delivery than the calculation allows.
  • the time period is broken down. on a project observed in the application, the estimate distinguished a preparation phase, internal review and external review, each encrypted separately. This breakdown is useful: it shows where time passes, and therefore what an arbitration can actually shorten.

The last point, often forgotten, is that when a batch has more than one file, the processing order is decided. If three out of twelve rooms condition your hearing, tell us at the time of quotation; a serious tunnel will ask you explicitly whether you want to set an order of priority.

6. Certification, notarisation, apostille: a separate case

Some documents not only require a rewritten translation, but a certified translation, sometimes notarized, sometimes apostilized. These three levels are not degrees of care: they are different formalities that engage different third parties.

  • Certification: the translation is accompanied by a signed and stamped certificate certifying its accuracy.
  • Notification: a Swiss notary is also appearing to authenticate the signature affixed to this certificate.
  • Apostille: the State Chancellery adds the apostille provided for in the Hague Convention of 5 October 1961 for the document to be effective abroad.

The time and circuit change at each step, as they depend on external players. Determine the level required before to start the translation, asking the authority or the receiving party: taking back a certification after the fact means starting again at the end of the chain.

7. Eight questions to ask before accepting an estimate

  1. What is your definition of the standard page? in characters or words, including spaces or not.
  2. What exactly does the proposed level of replay guarantee? ask for the written definition, not the commercial adjective.
  3. who connects, and with what specialization? a legal translator, a lawyer-linguist, an agency: this is not the same depending on the field.
  4. What happens if there is a terminological disagreement? who decides, on what source, and within what time frame.
  5. Where data is stored, and where it is processed? the two answers are distinct, and the second depends on the engine used.
  6. does the content feed a translation memory? If so, which one, accessible to whom, and can you disable it for a sensitive folder?
  7. What does the cost estimate cover if the source text changes along the way? a change to the original document after launch is the first drift factor.
  8. in what form is the deliverable returned? retained original format, bilingual document, work file: this will decide how long you take back from home.

The four quality levels and the service of certified commercial registry extracts cover the rest of the spectrum up to the apostille.

Frequently asked Questions

What is a standard translation page?

A standard page is a conventional counting unit based on the volume of text, not the number of sheets in the document. A file of ten highly ventilated sheets may represent fewer standard pages than a contract of three dense sheets. The exact definition, in terms of number of characters or words, varies from provider to provider: ask for it in writing, as it makes two estimates comparable.

Why does a quote ask for the legal category of the document?

Because it determines the reference terminology, the profile of the relector and therefore the processing time. In Corrext, the application detects the language and category of the document at the time of filing, for example private Insurance, and the user can correct this detection before the estimate is calculated.

What is the difference between the light review, full review and double review?

Light Review corrects material errors, omissions and contradictions: the result is complete and accurate on the merits, recommended to understand and for internal dissemination. Full review also includes consistency, idiomatism, style and tone for a third party document. Dual Review and Certified Translation shall have the set read together by two qualified legal translators, with a certificate confirming accuracy and impartiality, for legal use, publication or filing with an authority.

Is internal review free of charge?

It is not invoiced by the service provider, but it consumes legal time. That is why Corrext asks, in the internal review specifications, to specify the price per standard page that you will invoice yourself for this review: the estimate then shows a total cost that includes your own work, rather than leaving it invisible.

What questions do I have before accepting a legal translation quote?

Ask for the definition of the standard page, which exactly is guaranteed by the chosen level of replay, which connects and specializes, what happens in case of terminological disagreement, where the data is stored and processed, if the content feeds a translation memory, which is understood if the source text changes after launch, and in what format the deliverable is rendered.
