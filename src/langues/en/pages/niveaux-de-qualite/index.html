Machine Translation

Post-Edition

Frequently asked questions about quality levels

What is the difference between post-editing and legal review?

The post-editing is the adoption of automatic translation by a legal translator: in light reviews, material errors, omissions and contradictions are corrected; in full review, the text is also adapted for consistency, idiomatic turns, style, and tone. The legal review of level 3 corresponds to the double review and certified translation: the document is fully reproduced by two qualified legal translators from a specialized agency, who issues a signed certificate attesting to the accuracy and impartiality of the translation.

What is a certified translation in Switzerland?

It is a translation accompanied by a signed certificate certifying its accuracy and impartiality. In Corrext, this certificate is issued by the specialised agency in charge of the project, at the double review and certified translation level. According to the addressee, the certificate must then be authenticated by a Swiss notary, then accompanied by an apostille from the State Chancellery under the Hague Convention of 5 October 1961. The requirements vary from authority to canton: the recipient authority remains the judge of the required level.

Can I change my level during the project?

The level is chosen file by file in the Project Management quotes step, before generating the quote: you can compare levels and deadlines as often as necessary until the quote is generated. Once the project is launched, a level change is handled with the team, as it engages an external relector and a new time estimate.

Who are the reviewers?

For the light Review and full Review, a legal translator. For the dual review and certified translation, a specialised translation agency that employs two qualified legal translators and signs the certificate. For extracts from the commercial register: legal translators specialising in corporate safekeeping. And if you choose Internal Review, it's your own teams, whose work is valued in the quote.

How long does each level take?

The delay depends on the number of standard pages, the legal category detected, the level of review, and the desired due date. Corrext displays it in the quote before any order, distinguishing preparation, internal review, and external review. Level 1, Machine-only Translation, is immediate in text and document Translation.

Can I have my own teams read?

Yes. The Internal Review entrusts the review to your organization: you specify the number of standard pages concerned, the price per standard page charged by your team and its approximate hourly price, and Corrext values this work in the quote next to the estimated replay time. This is the way to compare internal and external reinterpretations on figures.
